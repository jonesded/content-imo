# HNM Devs Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

At HNM Devs, we are committed to fostering an inclusive, welcoming, and innovative community where developers of all backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives can collaborate, learn, and build amazing things together.

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- **Being Respectful**: Treating all community members with courtesy and professionalism
- **Being Inclusive**: Welcoming newcomers and helping them feel at home
- **Being Collaborative**: Working together constructively and sharing knowledge freely
- **Being Constructive**: Providing helpful feedback and focusing on solutions
- **Being Patient**: Understanding that learning takes time and everyone progresses differently
- **Being Open-Minded**: Embracing different approaches and perspectives
- **Giving Credit**: Acknowledging others' contributions and ideas
- **Taking Responsibility**: Owning mistakes and learning from them

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Offensive, derogatory, or discriminatory comments or jokes
- Personal attacks, trolling, or inflammatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Disrupting discussions or events
- Spamming or excessive self-promotion
- Encouraging or engaging in any of the above behaviors

## Our Responsibilities

### Community Leaders

Community leaders are responsible for:

- Clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior
- Taking appropriate and fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior
- Removing, editing, or rejecting contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct
- Communicating reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate

### Community Members

All community members are expected to:

- Follow this Code of Conduct in all community spaces
- Report violations to the community leaders
- Support fellow community members
- Help maintain a positive environment

## Enforcement

### Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:

- **Email**: conduct@hnmdevs.com
- **Discord**: Direct message to any moderator
- **GitHub**: Create a private issue or contact maintainers

All reports will be handled confidentially and promptly. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of the reporter.

### Enforcement Process

1. **Assessment**: Reports are reviewed within 24 hours
2. **Investigation**: We gather all relevant information
3. **Decision**: Appropriate action is determined based on severity
4. **Communication**: All parties are informed of the outcome
5. **Follow-up**: We ensure the situation is resolved

### Consequences

Violations may result in:

- **Warning**: Private conversation about the behavior
- **Temporary Suspension**: Temporary ban from community spaces
- **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from the community
- **Legal Action**: In cases involving illegal activity

The severity of consequences depends on the nature and context of the violation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All HNM Devs community spaces (GitHub, Discord, forums, events)
- Public spaces when representing the HNM Devs community
- Private communications related to community business
- Online and offline events hosted by HNM Devs

## Community Guidelines

### Inclusive Language

We encourage:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what's best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

### Technical Discussions

When participating in technical discussions:

- Stay on topic and provide context
- Share resources and document sources
- Be patient with questions at all levels
- Encourage experimentation and learning
- Celebrate both successes and learning from failures

### Mentorship Culture

We believe in:

- **Knowledge Sharing**: Freely sharing expertise and resources
- **Peer Learning**: Learning from each other regardless of experience level
- **Growth Mindset**: Embracing challenges as opportunities to improve
- **Community Building**: Actively helping others succeed

## Recognition and Appreciation

We celebrate community members who:

- Help others learn and grow
- Contribute high-quality content and code
- Demonstrate leadership and mentorship
- Foster inclusive community culture
- Go above and beyond to support the community
